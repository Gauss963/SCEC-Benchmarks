          Rate-and-State Friction, Ageing Law, Half-Space
This problem is identical to TPV101, but the fault is embedded in a half-space rather than
a whole-space. In terms of the coordinate system defined for TPV101, the half-space is
the region y > 0 . The plane y = 0 is a free surface.
